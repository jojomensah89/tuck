Tuck

Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Tuck is a desktop app for Windows. It has no account and no analytics, and nothing it keeps is sent to the developer. This page says what stays on your PC, what leaves it and why, and what this website counts.

What stays on your PC

  • Clipboard history is kept in memory and is gone when Tuck closes. With Keep after restart on, it is saved in Tuck’s own app data, capped at 256 MB. Turning history off deletes it.
  • The file shelf, timers, reminders and settings are stored in Tuck’s app data folder on your PC.
  • Dictation uses the microphone only while you record. Speech is turned into text on your processor, and the audio is not uploaded.
  • Notification previews are off until you turn them on. When on, Tuck reads Windows notifications through the permission you grant and shows them on the island. They are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Media and system readings (what is playing, battery, CPU and memory) are read locally and shown on the island.
  • Microphone and camera indicator reads from Windows which apps are using your microphone or camera, only to show the dot. Tuck keeps and sends nothing from it. Turn it off in Settings > Privacy and Tuck stops reading.

What leaves your PC, and when

  • Lyrics. With lyrics on, the title, artist and length of the playing track go to LRCLIB, a free community lyrics library.
  • Speech models. When you pick a dictation model, it is downloaded from Hugging Face. After that, dictation works offline.
  • AI usage. If you switch on Claude or Codex usage, their own apps report your limits to Tuck and may contact Anthropic or OpenAI. For Antigravity, only after you confirm, Tuck contacts Google for your quota.
  • Updates. The version downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub for a new release. The Microsoft Store version does not; the Store updates it.

Nothing else. Tuck contains no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting.

This website

tuck.jojomensah.com counts visits with a self-hosted counter. It sets two first-party cookies, one for the visitor and one for the session, and records the page, the referring site and which buttons are clicked. It shares nothing with advertising networks. The app itself does not load the counter.
